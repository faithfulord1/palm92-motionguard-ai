# Palm92 MotionGuard AI Hackathon Pack

## One-line pitch
Personalised, explainable movement-risk intelligence that compares a person with their own baseline, keeps humans in control and preserves the evidence behind every decision.

## Problem
Movement and posture guidance is often generic. Wearable signals can produce large volumes of data, but a useful safety system must translate those signals into understandable patterns without pretending that sensor correlation proves the medical cause of pain.

## Solution
MotionGuard normalises wearable data, compares it with an individual's baseline, detects explainable movement-risk patterns, shows a body/movement view, and routes consequential flags to an authorised human reviewer. The full chain is auditable.

## Demo story
1. Start a synthetic wearable session.
2. Watch live flexion, twist, asymmetry, repetition and static-posture readings.
3. Trigger a movement deviation.
4. Show the body visualisation and personal-baseline comparison.
5. Open the explainable alert to show observation vs inference.
6. Enter the Human Review Centre and record a decision plus rationale.
7. Show the immutable evidence timeline.
8. Generate/print the session report.
9. Show governance and MCP/wearable integration architecture.

## Differentiators
- Personal baseline first, generic threshold second
- Explainable flags rather than opaque risk scores
- Observation, inference and human decision kept separate
- Human-in-the-loop review with rationale
- Evidence-by-design audit trail
- Synthetic-data demo, avoiding fake clinical claims
- MCP-ready governed tool surface
- Hardware-agnostic wearable adapter contract
- Safety and occupational-health expansion path

## 60-second pitch
"Most movement tools compare everyone with the same rule. Palm92 MotionGuard AI starts with the individual. It turns wearable movement signals into a personal baseline, detects meaningful changes, explains exactly what triggered each flag, and sends consequential findings to a human reviewer. The AI never gets to rewrite its own history: the original sensor observation, AI inference and human decision remain visible in the evidence trail. Our current prototype uses synthetic wearable data, so we can demonstrate the full workflow without pretending to have clinical validation. MotionGuard is designed for future physiotherapy decision support, occupational health, ageing and mobility, care, rail, construction and workplace safety. AI investigates. Humans decide. Evidence proves why."
